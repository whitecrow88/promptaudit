## 🧩 Purpose
Ensure prompts and supporting docs are **accurate, unambiguous, and agent-ready.**
This template guides reviewers to produce structured, actionable audit reports.

---

## 1. Summary of Issues
List contradictions, unclear instructions, or missing context.

---

## 2. Analysis / Context
Explain **why** the issues exist — tone drift, unclear acceptance criteria, etc.

---

## 3. Suggested Rewrites / Additions
Provide improved sections or prompt examples in markdown/code fences.

---

## 4. Confidence + Verification Steps
- **Confidence:** High / Medium / Low  
- **Next verification:** (e.g. test in sandbox agent, review with designer)

---

## 5. Recommendations & Fixes (Prioritised)
| Priority | Area | Recommendation | Rationale | Confidence |
|-----------|------|----------------|------------|-------------|
| 🔴 High | ... | ... | ... | High |
| 🟠 Medium | ... | ... | ... | Medium |
| 🟢 Low | ... | ... | ... | High |

**Priority Key:**  
🔴 Must fix before next iteration  
🟠 Should fix soon  
🟢 Optional / minor improvement
